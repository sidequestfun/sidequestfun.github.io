Why Sidequests Beat Boredom

It's time to stop living the same day over and over and start adding new chapters to your story. Do you remember when life felt like a grand adventure? Somewhere along the way, routines took over. Sidequests are here to bring that magic back. By embracing small, unexpected challenges, you disrupt the monotony and trigger new ways of thinking.

Every sidequest is a playful micro-adventure. In a world of endless scrolling, we wanted to build a tool that encourages real-world interaction. There are no accounts, no ads, and no hidden costs. Just free sidequests for everyone!

Privacy Policy

1. General Information

This website is a private, non-commercial hobby project for entertainment purposes only. The content is provided "as is" without any warranties. Any liability for damages of any kind is strictly disclaimed.

When you access this website, technically necessary data (such as your IP address) are processed by GitHub as the hosting provider.

2. Local Storage (Progress Saving)

To provide the functionality to save quests, add your own custom quests and track your completed "Sidequests", this website utilizes the LocalStorage of your browser.

  • Data Residency: Data (such as your saved progress and counters) is stored exclusively on your own device.
  • No Transmission: This data is not transmitted to our servers or any third parties.
  • Control: You can delete this data at any time by clearing your browser's cache or website data in your browser settings.

3. External Services & CDNs

To ensure a smooth experience and provide visual elements (icons, fonts, and effects), this website loads external libraries from Content Delivery Networks (CDNs). These include:

  • FontAwesome (Icons)
  • jsDelivr (Canvas-Confetti effect)
  • LZ-String (for share links)

Technical Necessity: When these resources are loaded, your IP address is technically transmitted to these providers to deliver the files to your browser. This is a technical requirement for the website to function correctly.

4. Web Analytics (Cloudflare)

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how many people visit our site. This service is privacy-focused and does not use cookies or collect personal data that can identify you. It only provides us with anonymous, aggregated statistics (e.g., number of visitors, country).

5. Sharing Custom Quests

When you use the "Share" function, a unique link is generated that contains your quest data in an encoded format. No data is stored on our end during this process; the information is contained entirely within the URL you share.

Note on Link Sharing: Since quest data is stored directly within the URL, sharing via link is optimized for up to 50 quests. For larger decks, please use the ⁠.json⁠ file export ("Export as File") to prevent messaging apps like WhatsApp from truncating long links.
